Publications

Uncertainty- and risk-gated residual reinforcement learning with re-distillation for long-tail interaction learning in camera-based end-to-end autonomous racing

Hyeonseo Oh*, Hyunjun Choi  *corresponding author

KSAE Fall Conference 2026under review

Treats RL as a transient teacher rather than a deployed component. A three-part gate (distance, MC-Dropout epistemic uncertainty, TTC-based risk) decides when the residual RL policy may intervene; only gated segments are re-distilled into the imitation-learning base over 10 rounds, after which RL is removed. On a 10-minute multi-car scenario the distilled model shows 33% fewer collisions and 24% higher average speed than the residual-RL system, with 3.3x more overtaking attempts.

Safety-aware trajectory re-ranking for multimodal end-to-end autonomous driving planners

Hyeonseo Oh, Chungman Kwon, Woongje Jo, Siho Kim

KSAE Spring Conference 2026, poster, paper no. 26SKSAEJ0222

A three-stage external safety layer that verifies a planner's output trajectories without modifying or retraining it: clearance/TTC hard rejection, soft re-scoring, and an emergency-deceleration fallback. Evaluated open-loop on Autoware logs first, then closed-loop in CARLA on 30 randomized cut-in scenarios; collision rate 100% to 0%, peak deceleration 15.0 to 1.8 m/s², with sensitivity analysis over the thresholds.

Knowledge-graph-based semantic scene understanding for autonomous driving in dilemma situations

Woongje Jo, Hyeonseo Oh, Junseok Lee, Siho Kim

KSAE Spring Conference 2026, paper no. 26SKSAE_J023

Encodes traffic law, physics, and defensive-driving common sense as an OWL ontology (GraphDB, 811 asserted / 1,498 inferred triples) and compares LLM scene reasoning against a 3D scene graph under identical perception input. Mean score 4.41 vs 3.56 over 300 trials (p < 0.001); ablations show the gain comes mainly from natural-language semantic descriptions rather than ontology structure alone.
